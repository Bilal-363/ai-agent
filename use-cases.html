Built for the way your practice actually runs.

By practice type

Dental Practices

Single-location dental practices lose more revenue to unanswered hygiene recall than almost anything else. Casey works your recall list and answers every new-patient call on the first ring.

  • Hygiene recall worked automatically, every week
  • New-patient calls answered before they call the practice down the street
  • Emergency triage with same-day slot offers
  • Native Dentrix, Open Dental, Curve, and NexHealth support
Typical outcome: Practices typically recover 15–25 hygiene appointments a month that were previously lost to voicemail.

By practice type

Primary Care

Primary care call volume is relentless and mostly routine. Casey resolves the routine and escalates the rest with proper documentation.

  • Refill requests captured and routed with the medication list attached
  • Annual wellness and chronic-care follow-up scheduling
  • Structured telephone encounters written to the chart
  • Epic, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, NextGen, Practice Fusion
Typical outcome: Front-desk staff report getting back 2+ hours a day for in-person patient care.

By practice type

Multi-Location Groups

Casey enforces the same scheduling rules and the same standard of service across every site, and gives you one dashboard to see all of it.

  • Location-aware routing and per-site scheduling rules
  • Consolidated reporting across every clinic
  • Load-balances patients to the nearest site with availability
  • Roll out to one location, then clone the configuration
Typical outcome: Groups get one consistent patient experience without hiring per-site reception staff.

By practice type

DSOs

For dental service organisations, Casey is a central patient-access layer that scales with acquisitions instead of requiring a hiring round for each one.

  • Central configuration with per-practice overrides
  • New acquisitions live in days, not quarters
  • Portfolio-level KPIs: answer rate, conversion, recall performance
  • Predictable per-location cost with no turnover risk
Typical outcome: Onboard an acquired practice’s phone line in under a week.

By practice type

Specialty Clinics

Specialty practices live or die on clean referral intake. Casey captures the referral, chases the records, and prepares the patient.

  • Referral intake with referring-provider capture
  • Pre-visit instructions and prep confirmation calls
  • Long-cycle follow-up sequences for procedures
  • Prior-authorisation status calls and reminders
Typical outcome: Fewer visits cancelled on the day for missing records or missed prep.

By practice type

FQHCs & Community Health

Community health centres carry the highest call volume and the most complex patient needs. Casey answers in the patient’s language, every time.

  • 20+ languages with no separate translation line
  • Sliding-scale and eligibility questions answered consistently
  • Handles very high inbound volume without a queue
  • Transportation and interpreter needs flagged in advance
Typical outcome: Language is no longer the reason a patient gives up on calling.

By job to be done

Front Desk Relief

Your front desk was never meant to be a call centre. Casey takes the phone so your team can look after the people standing in front of them.

  • Absorbs the routine 70% of inbound call volume
  • No more choosing between the phone and the patient at the desk
  • Zero turnover, zero retraining, zero sick days
  • Staff keep full oversight through the dashboard
Typical outcome: Two-plus hours of admin time back, per staff member, per day.

By job to be done

Missed-Call Recovery

67% of callers hang up after two minutes on hold. Casey makes sure there is never a second minute.

  • Answers in under two seconds, every time, all day
  • Overflow coverage for peak hours and lunch
  • Calls back anyone who abandons before connecting
  • Recovers after-hours and weekend demand
Typical outcome: 87% fewer missed calls in the first month.

By job to be done

No-Show Reduction

A no-show costs more than the visit. Casey reduces them with real conversations, not one-way texts nobody reads.

  • Multi-touch reminder cadence across voice and SMS
  • Patients can reschedule mid-reminder instead of ghosting
  • Automatic waitlist backfill for late cancellations
  • No-show risk surfaced to the practice manager weekly
Typical outcome: No-show rates held under 5% for reminded patients.

By job to be done

Patient Reactivation

The cheapest new patient is the one you already had. Casey works your lapsed list with warm, personal outbound calls.

  • Outbound campaigns to patients overdue by 6, 12, or 24 months
  • Personalised by last visit type and provider
  • Books straight into open capacity you want to fill
  • Full opt-out handling and contact-preference compliance
Typical outcome: A reactivation campaign typically pays for the year in a single month.
